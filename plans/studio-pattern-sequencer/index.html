Pattern Sequencer 2.0

implementationstudio-pattern-sequencer · 2026-09-28 · annotate below, then hit “Save annotations”

Goal

Turn SESSION's temporary 3×16 drum scratchpad into an editable pattern instrument. Producers can create or reopen drum tracks, program six lanes with per-step velocity, choose a kit, set swing, and use one, two, or four bars. The pattern remains the channel's shared source, so every playlist placement updates together; audition, playback, stems, exports, save, recovery, Undo/Redo, and collaboration all preserve the same deterministic rhythm.

Non-goals

  • User-uploaded drum samples, slicing, round robin layers, and per-lane effect chains are separate releases.
  • Polyrhythmic lane lengths, probability, ratchets, triplet grids, and live MIDI pad recording remain outside this bounded release.
  • Legacy 3×16 tracks retain their exact eight-bar sound and duration until a producer explicitly upgrades or edits them.

Screens & flows

Editable drum channel

Select any modern drum track and open Drum sequencer to edit its source. Header controls choose kit, bars, and swing; Apply is one Undo step and Cancel leaves the arrangement untouched.

Velocity step grid

Six horizontally scrollable lanes cover kick, snare, clap, closed hat, open hat, and percussion. Click cycles Off/Soft/Medium/Full; exact velocity controls the selected step.

Audition and playlist reuse

Audition the draft before applying it. The applied pattern renders once as the channel source and every duplicated or repeated playlist placement hears the update.

Risks

HIGHChanging the old sequence semantics could alter existing songs. Keep the legacy array and renderer untouched; versioned patterns use a separate field and explicit upgrade action.
MEDSwing or multi-bar rounding can drift across repeats. Derive all timings from integer step indices and bar duration, then verify bar-aligned output and playlist placement length.
MEDLarge grids can overwhelm phone layout and collaboration merges. Bound patterns to 64 steps, scroll within the editor, use touch-sized cells, and merge stable lane/step coordinates.
