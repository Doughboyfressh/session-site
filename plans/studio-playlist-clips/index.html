Studio playlist clips

Goal

Let producers arrange a song from reusable clip blocks without creating a new mixer channel for every repeat. Every audio, MIDI, drum, sample, and demo source can have multiple named placements on its channel; each placement can be moved, trimmed, split, duplicated, repeated, and removed while sharing the channel mix and automation. The release succeeds when the same arrangement is heard in playback and rendered exports, survives recovery and collaboration, remains compatible with older projects, and is practical on phone and desktop.

Non-goals

  • Elastic audio warping, transient slicing, pitch correction, and destructive audio consolidation are separate releases.
  • Plugin hosting, third-party VST/AU loading, and a full FL Studio pattern picker are outside this release.
  • Clip-level effect chains and automation stay channel-wide for now; fades and timing remain clip-specific.

Screens & flows

Duplicate a card per screen or flow. Sketch the shape in words; wireframes can come later.

Arrangement timeline

Each mixer channel row can show many distinct clip blocks. A click selects one block and seeks to it; the selected state remains clear without losing channel focus.

Clip inspector

Rename, move, trim, fade, split, duplicate, repeat, and remove the selected placement. Beat snapping and one-step Undo apply to every edit.

Playback and export

All placements use one decoded source and one mixer channel. Live playback, looping, processed mixdown, and dry stems render the same clip layout.

Save and collaboration

Legacy projects open as one primary clip. New placements recover from local drafts, save to the project, and merge by stable clip identity with understandable conflicts.

Risks

HIGHScheduling channel automation once per clip would create conflicting AudioParam events. Separate channel automation scheduling from per-placement source and fade scheduling, then prove it in the PCM harness.
HIGHMalformed or huge placement arrays could increase CPU and saved-project size. Sanitize every field and cap the model at 32 placements per channel and 256 per project.
MEDA selected clip can disappear after collaboration or Undo. Reconcile selection by stable ID and fall back to the primary clip without crashing.
MEDDense timelines can become unusable on phones. Keep horizontal scrolling, minimum touch targets, concise labels, and a dedicated inspector with tested mobile behavior.
