SESSION social collaboration loop

release validatedsocial-collaboration-loop · 2026-09-27 · annotate below, then hit “Save annotations”

Goal

Help artists, producers, and engineers move from discovering relevant music to starting a private collaboration and following its progress without leaving SESSION. The loop works when a member can find a suitable public track, send a role-specific request, accept it, exchange messages, and see each important event in one activity inbox.

Non-goals

  • Payments, subscriptions, licensing checkout, royalty accounting, and music distribution.
  • Public livestreaming, message attachments, group chats, and an opaque machine-learning recommendation service.
  • Expanding the browser DAW or changing existing studio-room audio and video behavior.

Screens & flows

Duplicate a card per screen or flow. Sketch the shape in words; wireframes can come later.

Discover music

For You, Following, and New tabs rank public tracks with a clear reason such as “From someone you follow” or “Matches your saved R&B tracks.” Existing search and role filters remain available.

Request collaboration

A track or creator opens a focused request form with the intended role, optional public-track context, and a concise introduction. The sender sees the request as pending immediately.

Collaboration inbox

Incoming and sent requests show pending, accepted, declined, or closed status. Accepted requests become private text conversations tied to their original track and participants.

Activity

A compact timeline covers follows, comments, requests, decisions, and new messages. Unread counts appear in navigation; members can mark one item or all items read.

Risks

HIGHPrivate messages could leak through weak authorization. Every read and write checks the signed-in user against request participants, and all social responses use private no-store caching.
MEDActivity events could duplicate or become noisy. Events use stable dedupe keys for repeatable actions, suppress self-notifications, and keep bounded copy.
MEDFeed ranking could feel arbitrary. SESSION returns one plain-language reason for each recommendation and offers chronological Following and New alternatives.
